# 24h marathon — full text plan

## Score target math
1300 total = 650 RW + 650 Math. Digital SAT is adaptive:
- Each section: Module 1 → Module 2 (Easy or Hard).
- To unlock Hard Module 2, get roughly **60%+ of Module 1 right**.
- Hard Module 2 is where 650+ is earned.

Realistic day targets:
- Easy/Medium errors: drive to near-zero — they cap Module 2 routing.
- High-yield types (marked 🔥 in Types tab): aim for 85%+ accuracy under time.

## Hour-by-hour (24h marathon)
**0:00–1:00** — Diagnostic: 10 RW + 10 Math from Drills, mixed, timed. Tag 3 weakest types and 3 most-missed traps.
**1:00–5:00** — Math deep block: Types 45 min (M1, M2, M3, M4, M5), then 3 drills per type (Medium), then 1 Hard-only set. Flashcards between sets.
**5:00–9:00** — RW deep block: Types 45 min (RW8, RW9, RW10, RW1, RW7), then 3 drills per type, then Hard-only set.
**9:00–11:00** — Drill set timed, Hard only, 25-min blocks; flag everything you'd guess. 
**11:00–12:00** — Review misses. Write each trap ID once. Flashcards miss-only.
**12:00–20:00** — Sleep. Non-negotiable. Do not study.
**20:00–21:00** — Mini-Mock #1 (35 min, 20Q). Review every miss 30 min.
**21:00–22:30** — Weak-type drills only. No new topics.
**22:30–23:30** — Mini-Mock #2. Review 20 min.
**23:30–24:00** — Formulas print + Traps + Desmos vault. Pack exam-day bag. Stop screens 30 min before sleep.

## What to avoid today
- No new topic that isn't in the 14 Math / 12 RW types tab.
- No full Bluebook 3-hour test after midnight.
- No re-reading notes — drills and flashcards only.
